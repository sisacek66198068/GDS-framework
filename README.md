# GDS — Gravitational Dynamical System

**A Unified Dynamical Field Framework for Cosmology Without Dark Matter and Dark Energy**

GDS (Gravitational Dynamical System) is an independent theoretical and computational research framework investigating whether gravitational and cosmological phenomena conventionally attributed to dark matter and dark energy can be reinterpreted through nonlinear dynamical field behaviour.

The project combines theoretical development, numerical modelling, observational comparison and reproducible computational validation.

---

## Research Objective

The central research question of GDS is:

> Can gravitational systems exhibit emergent nonlinear dynamical behaviour capable of reproducing phenomena conventionally attributed to additional unseen matter or cosmological energy components?

GDS investigates this question through explicit mathematical models, numerical experiments and comparison with observational datasets.

The framework is presented as a research hypothesis and computational programme, not as an established replacement for the standard cosmological model.

---

## Research Areas

Current GDS research includes:

- Cosmological dynamics
- Nonlinear gravitational fields
- Structure formation
- Galactic dynamics
- Gravitational collapse
- Near-horizon dynamics
- Cosmological expansion
- Matter-density evolution
- Dynamical stability and metastability
- Comparison with ΛCDM
- Observational and numerical validation

## GIGAALL Validation

The GIGAALL programme provides a combined cosmological benchmark for comparing GDS/GSC-based models with reference cosmological configurations.

The validation portfolio includes combinations of:

- cosmological expansion constraints
- matter-density evolution
- structure-related observables
- Hubble parameter scans
- σ8 parameter scans
- CMB-related constraints
- BAO-related constraints
- alternative dynamical configurations

Detailed results and limitations are documented in the corresponding research artifacts and publications.

## Gravitational Collapse

A dedicated branch of the project investigates nonlinear dynamical evolution during gravitational collapse.

This includes research into:

- density evolution
- horizon-scale dynamics
- structural transitions
- mass transfer
- saturation mechanisms
- dynamical stability
- compact-object behaviour

Associated figures, numerical results and research material are included in this repository.

---

## Observational Research

GDS is developed through comparison between theoretical predictions and observational or reference cosmological data.

The research programme is designed around reproducibility:

**Theory → Mathematical Model → Numerical Simulation → Observable Prediction → Dataset Comparison → Validation**

Disagreement with observational evidence is treated as a constraint on the framework rather than removed through post-hoc interpretation.

---

## Relationship to GSC-X

GDS and GSC-X represent two connected but distinct research programmes.

**GDS** investigates the underlying nonlinear dynamics of gravitational and cosmological systems.

**GSC-X (Generative Stability Control)** develops a general Executive architecture for monitoring, prediction, adaptive intervention and stability management across complex dynamical systems.

The two projects share a common interest in dynamical stability, metastability and emergent system behaviour while maintaining separate theoretical and engineering objectives.

## Scientific Scope

GDS is an active independent research programme.

Results presented in this repository should be interpreted within the assumptions, datasets and numerical conditions documented for each experiment.

The framework proposes testable hypotheses and computational models. Its conclusions remain subject to further observational validation, independent reproduction and scientific review.
